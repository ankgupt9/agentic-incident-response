## Symptoms
p95 latency of POST /orders rises. The error rate stays flat and submissions still return 202.

## Likely causes
1. The quote call is slow but still under the timeout.
2. order-service is under CPU or connection pressure.

## How to confirm
Compare the latency of the quote call with total submit latency using a trace. If the quote dominates, pricing-service is slow.

## Fix options
Investigate saturation on pricing-service and scale it if it is CPU bound.

## Escalate when
Latency approaches the client timeout.
