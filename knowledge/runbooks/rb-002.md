## Symptoms
POST /orders returns 502 for every order. order-service logs a quote request failing with `503 Service Unavailable`. The submission error alert fires. The consumer sees no new orders.

## Likely causes
1. pricing-service is unavailable or failing every request.
2. Its upstream price source is unreachable.
3. A bad release of pricing-service.

## How to confirm
Call the quote endpoint for several SKUs directly. A 503 on every SKU means the service is failing as a whole, not a single missing item. Check for a recent deploy of pricing-service in the change records and look at its error logs.

## Fix options
Roll back a recent deploy, or restart the service. Raising the order service timeout does not help because the failure is an error, not slowness.

## Escalate when
The service does not recover after a restart or rollback.
