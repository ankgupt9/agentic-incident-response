## Symptoms
Panels show `No data` or a datasource error, although the services are running.

## Likely causes
1. The datasource is unreachable or misconfigured.
2. The query window or label selector no longer matches the data.

## How to confirm
Open the panel's query, run it in Explore, and check the datasource health page.

## Fix options
Fix the datasource URL or credentials, then reload the dashboard.

## Escalate when
Several datasources fail at once.
