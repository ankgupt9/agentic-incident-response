## Symptoms
POST /orders returns 502 only for orders that name a particular SKU. pricing-service logs `quote requested for unknown sku=...` and the quote endpoint returns 404 for that SKU.

## Likely causes
1. The item was removed from the pricing catalog (for example by a catalog sync) while orders still reference it.
2. The client sends a wrong or retired SKU.

## How to confirm
List which SKUs fail and check whether they are all missing from the catalog. Look for a recent data change on pricing-service, such as a catalog sync removing items.

## Fix options
Restore the item in the catalog (needs approval), or stop upstream clients from ordering it.

## Escalate when
Many SKUs are missing, which suggests a bad catalog sync.
