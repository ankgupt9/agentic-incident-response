## Symptoms
Orders return 202 and then never complete. The pricing consumer logs `failed to price order` with a ValueError saying `price mismatch for SKU-x: quoted A, current B`. The consumer error alert fires while submissions look healthy. Processed-ok falls to zero while processed-error rises.

## Likely causes
1. The quote endpoint is serving prices that differ from the price list the consumer validates against, for example because the quote cache is not refreshing or its refresh interval was lengthened.
2. A price list update reached one path but not the other.
3. A short mismatch while a price list update is in flight (clears by itself).

## How to confirm
Compare quoted and current prices in several rejected orders. If the ratio is the same across different SKUs, the quote source has drifted systematically, which points to the cache or its configuration. If only some SKUs differ, suspect a data change instead. Then look at recent changes for pricing-service (cache refresh, configuration, catalog sync) and read the service catalog entry for how quotes are produced.

## Fix options
Refresh or flush the quote cache so quotes match the price list (needs approval). Replay the rejected orders in the sandbox after the fix. Do not loosen the consumer's price check as a stopgap.

## Escalate when
Only some SKUs mismatch, or the mismatch continues after a cache refresh.
