## Symptoms
The broker reports a disk alarm and stops accepting messages. Publishers block.

## Likely causes
1. Persistent messages accumulated on disk.
2. Logs or temporary files filled the volume.

## How to confirm
Check free space on the broker volume and the size of the largest queues.

## Fix options
Free space by draining queues or clearing old logs. Add capacity if the growth is steady.

## Escalate when
Free space keeps falling after cleanup.
