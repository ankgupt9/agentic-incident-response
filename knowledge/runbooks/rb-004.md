## Symptoms
The consumer logs `failed to price order` with a KeyError for a required field such as sku, or with a JSON decode error. Rejected messages share the same shape. The consumer error alert fires while submissions return 202.

## Likely causes
1. The producer changed the message shape (a renamed field) without the consumer being updated.
2. The producer is publishing truncated or cut-off payloads.

## How to confirm
Read the exception type. A KeyError for a field points to a renamed or missing field; a decode error points to a cut-off or invalid body. Compare the failing message with the order-message contract and look for a recent schema or release change on order-service.

## Fix options
Revert the producer change, or update the consumer to accept both shapes. Replay the rejected orders in the sandbox once fixed.

## Escalate when
Messages from before and after the change are mixed in the queue, or the cause is not a producer change.
