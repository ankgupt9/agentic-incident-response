## Symptoms
POST /orders returns 502. The quote request succeeds (200) but order-service then logs an AMQP connection error while publishing. Submission errors rise; the queue stops receiving orders.

## Likely causes
1. order-service lost its connection to RabbitMQ and cannot publish.
2. RabbitMQ restarted or is unreachable from order-service.

## How to confirm
Confirm the error text names the broker connection, not the pricing call. Check broker health and the number of connections. If the broker is healthy, the problem is the order service's connection.

## Fix options
Restart order-service so it re-establishes its connection. Check the broker logs before restarting the broker.

## Escalate when
The broker is down, or connections do not recover after a restart.
