## Symptoms
Clients log TLS handshake failures or `certificate expired` when connecting to the broker.

## Likely causes
1. The listener certificate passed its expiry date.

## How to confirm
Check the certificate validity dates on the broker listener.

## Fix options
Renew and deploy the certificate, then restart the listener.

## Escalate when
Renewal is blocked or the certificate authority is unreachable.
